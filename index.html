      Temperature compensation

      --:--

      Rule of thumb: about 9% per °C, shorter when warmer. Good between 18 and 24 °C. Use your datasheet’s table when it has one, and keep C-41 at its own fixed temperature.

      Push and pull

      --:--

      A rough guide only: pushes add about 30% per stop, pulls take off about 20%. Check the datasheet or a trusted chart for your film and developer.
